Avocado Smoothie

Description

This avocado smoothie is thick, creamy, and delicious. Makes a great breakfast smoothie.

This smoothie combines creamy avocado with vanilla yogurt and honey for a rich, smooth texture.

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe avocado, halved and pitted
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup vanilla yogurt
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 8 ice cubes

Steps:

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Scoop avocado flesh into a blender; add milk, yogurt, honey, and ice cubes. Blend ingredients until smooth.
